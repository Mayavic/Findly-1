Our story

Two friends, one shared obsession

Findly is, above all, our story. We met during our studies, with the same love of beautiful pieces and the same (not always reasonable) cravings… and without knowing it, we were writing the first lines of a great friendship.

Since then, we’ve grown up together. We cut our teeth at major luxury houses, shared flats, trips, pets, life moments, fits of laughter… and a few obsessions. One of them never left us: the urge to get our hands on THE piece we had in mind, even if it meant hours of searching, fifteen tabs open and hundreds of links swapped.

So, after so many years of sharing our everyday lives, we decided to set off on an entrepreneurial adventure together.

That’s how Findly came to life. The tool we always dreamed of having. And that we decided to create for you.
